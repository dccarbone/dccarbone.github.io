# Privacy policy

This policy covers `gmail-497400`, a personal Google OAuth app. Only its owner, Diego Carbone, uses it.

- **What it accesses:** the owner's own Gmail accounts, through the `gmail.modify` scope. It reads the sender, subject and snippet of new inbox messages, then applies labels, archives or moves messages to trash.
- **Who sees the data:** nobody else. The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Message metadata goes to a classification model only to decide how to file each message. A short history of decisions stays on the owner's own server.
- **How to revoke access:** remove it at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
